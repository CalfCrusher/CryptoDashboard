## 🛡️ Risk Management Quick Rules

### Stop Loss
- **LONG**: Below recent swing low - (0.5 × ATR)
- **SHORT**: Above recent swing high + (0.5 × ATR)

### Take Profits
- **TP1**: 1.5 × ATR (close 50%)
- **TP2**: 2.5 × ATR (close 30%)
- **TP3**: 4.0 × ATR (let 20% run)

### Risk/Reward
- **Minimum**: 1:2
- **Good**: 1:3
- **Excellent**: 1:4+

### Position Sizing
```
Position = (Account × Risk%) / Stop Distance
Example: ($10,000 × 2%) / $1,500 = 0.133 BTC
```

### Max Risk Per Trade
- **Conservative**: 1%
- **Moderate**: 2%
- **Aggressive**: 3% (NOT recommended)

## 📊 Market Conditions Guide

### TRENDING MARKET (Trade actively)
- ADX > 25
- Clear higher highs/lows
- Volume confirming
- Momentum strong

### RANGING MARKET (WAIT)
- ADX < 20
- Price between MAs
- Low volatility
- Mixed signals

### BREAKOUT PENDING
- Bollinger Bands narrow
- ADX rising from <20
- Volume decreasing (consolidation)
- Wait for confirmation

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Best signals**: 7+ confluence, ADX >30, R:R >2.5
2. **Wait for pullbacks**: Don't chase green candles
3. **Volume confirms**: Always check volume trend
4. **Respect stops**: Never move stop away from entry
5. **Scale out**: Use TP1, TP2, TP3 plan
6. **Risk management**: Never >2% per trade
7. **Correlation**: Don't double up on BTC + ETH
8. **Timeframes**: Higher timeframes = more reliable
9. **Patience**: Wait signal is a valid signal
